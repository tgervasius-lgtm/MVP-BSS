## Rules

1. This register records options, triggers and recheck duties. It is not a purchase order, Vendor Lock or proof that an account/environment exists.
2. Current authorized incremental recurring commitment under issue #133 is `EUR 0`.
3. `Current cost` means BSS cash commitment, not list price, free-tier value or consumed credits. Actual invoices/subscriptions remain in the private finance system.
4. Exact prices, taxes, currencies, limits, commercial-use terms, regions, DPA/SLA and included usage are `UNVERIFIED` until checked in the vendor's current authoritative terms at the named gate.
5. Every paid activation requires an approved owner, ROI/risk reason, monthly and annual cost, activation date, cancellation/exit path and Decision Log reference.
6. A free plan is not assumed to remain sufficient. An open-source/self-hosted option includes hosting, patching, backup, monitoring, security and operator time.

## Paid activation record

Before any row changes from EUR 0 to a recurring commitment, append or link an approved decision containing:

- vendor, service, plan and environment;
- authoritative price, currency, tax treatment, monthly and annual estimate;
- free-tier limit and measured usage;
- concrete paid trigger and benefit over free/local alternatives;
- security, privacy, residency, DPA/SLA and support result;
- start date, owner, renewal/cancellation date and budget authority;
- export, migration, credential rotation and service-removal plan;
- affected roadmap gates and recheck date.

Do not store account identifiers, invoices, credentials, recovery data or private vendor access inventories in this public repository.
